How it works

The learner loop, in 6 steps

The same loop the header shows on each of these pages: sign in, take a lesson, walk it, carry the record away, have it checked, and share it if you choose.

  1. PlaceholderNo screenshot of this step yet

    Sign in

    Say whose training record this device holds, or sign a message with a wallet. The page lists every method, including the ones that fail closed.

  2. Choose a lesson

    Pick your trade’s course. Each lesson names the hall it stands in and every step it will ask of you.

  3. Walk it on campus

    Walk the hall on foot, read its walls, and sit in a seat scored by a rubric you can read before you start.

  4. Export your record

    Carry your record off this device as a file that carries its own digest.

  5. Verify it

    Anyone can recheck the file in a browser. The page says what a pass means, and what it does not.

  6. Share it

    Build a contribution under consent scopes you choose. The page itself sends nothing anywhere.

Trust and limits

What none of this is

The limits, stated once, plainly, where a visitor meets them.

NOT CERTIFICATIONNo seat, lesson or course here certifies anyone on anything. The simulators carry schematic physics and deterministic rubrics; passing one is practice, not a ticket. Where a trade has a real ticket it is issued by a jurisdiction, an employer or a hall, and this bundle is none of those.
NOT SIGNED OFF BY ANYONE WHO DOES THE WORK0 of 111 halls carry a practitioner sign-off. Every lesson here is unverified general practice, written to be argued with and corrected by journey-level practitioners from the halls it names - and none of them has reviewed a line of it yet.
THE SEATS COVER A THIN SLICE45 of the ladder's 3,663 rungs carry a simulator seat, and all 45 of those sit on a prerequisite chain with no seat anywhere in it. A rung with no seat is not partly covered; it is uncovered, and the ladder page draws the gaps as plainly as the coverage.
NOT A UNION ROSTERA taxonomy of skilled trades, not a roster of chartered locals. No real local is named and no real union's mark is drawn.
NOT SURVEYEDNo site has been surveyed and no real building is depicted. A campus is composed from a place's own character, and the registry says so in each record.

What a record is, and what it is not

Read word for word from the registry the verifier is built on, so the front door cannot promise more than the check behind it.

A verified record proves

  • the record has not changed since it was exported: its SHA-256 digest recomputes over the canonical form
  • every lesson, step, hall, seat, scenario, station and district it names exists in this bundle at the stated pack version
  • every step marked done whose kind writes a training episode carries that episode as evidence
  • no lesson is marked complete with a step still undone, and none ahead of its ladder prerequisite

It does not prove

  • who the learner is: identity.claimed is typed by the person, attested by this device only, and nothing checks it - unless a wallet signed, in which case it is the identity of a key, not of a person; a stolen key signs just as well
  • anything on any blockchain: a wallet signature is computed in the browser and carried in the file; nothing is written to any chain, nothing is anchored, and no one can look it up
  • that the device was honest: a record can be written by hand and will verify if it is internally consistent
  • that a self-reported step (walk, placard) happened at all
  • a station or crib mark is a device-local mark, not a recorded episode
  • that the seat was passed under supervision, or that any score means anything outside this bundle
  • any accreditation, ticket, licence or credential; no hall, employer or authority has signed anything
  • any duration of training, and no price is claimed

nothing here is an accreditation

How to read a claim here

Every record in this bundle carries one of five words for how it was come by. They are not decoration: a build refuses a record that claims the wrong one, and the suites check it.

RECORDEDtaken from a public source and cited where it is used
DERIVEDcomputed from something recorded, by a rule you can read
AUTHOREDwritten by us, and labelled as written rather than found
SCHEMATICthe shape of a thing, not a measurement of one
SCRIPTEDa hand-written deterministic policy, with no model behind it
