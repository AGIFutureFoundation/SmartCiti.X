# Languages

The Academy ships in **8 languages**. The catalogs live in
`i18n/locales/`, the loader in `i18n/catalog.mjs`, and `i18n/test.mjs` holds
every locale to structural parity with the English source and to agreement
with the packs (districts, strands, tiers, bands, modalities, states).

| Code | Language | English name | Direction | Status |

| `ar` | العربية | Arabic | rtl | machine-drafted, pending native review |
| `de` | Deutsch | German | ltr | machine-drafted, pending native review |
| `en` | English | English | ltr | source language |

| `pt` | Português | Portuguese | ltr | machine-drafted, pending native review |
| `zh` | 中文（简体） | Chinese (Simplified) | ltr | machine-drafted, pending native review |

The rendered surface is `web/trade_craft_languages.html`
(`python3 web/build_languages.py`) — the overview in every locale,
direction-aware, with figures read from the manifest.

**What is deliberately not translated:** the 111 hall names. Trade
names are load-bearing identity and stay in English until reviewed
trade-name translations exist; every locale says so in its own
`honesty.translation` note. Reviewed translations are a
[roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) milestone (v3.3).
