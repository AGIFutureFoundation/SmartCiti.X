# Custom spaces

A custom space is a training room composed only from what the bundle already
holds - seats, stations, tool cribs, props, furniture and signs - fitted to
the room of its strand and priced in declared figures:
6 spaces, 102 items.

## The process, for a rep planning a room

1. **Open [`web/trade_craft_spaces.html`](../web/trade_craft_spaces.html)** and pick a space.
2. **Read its purpose and the room it fits** - the room of its strand in every
   hall that would serve it.
3. **Open the item schedule.** Every item is one of: prop (62), furniture (11), seat (8), station (2), crib (5), sign (14), each read from
   its own registry. An item with no size says *footprint unknown* and its
   overlap is not checked.
4. **Read the protective equipment** - derived from the floor and wall the
   space stands on, never typed - and the predicted draw cost.
5. **Treat it as a proposal.** Its status is on every space and is quoted
   below.

## Check it offline

A space is a proposal, not a record: there is no verifier to run on a
learner's file here, and this page does not invent one. Its own suite is
`node spaces/test.mjs`.

## The honest limits

Quoted from the registry at build time, never retyped:

`spaces/registry/spaces.json#honesty.status`:

> declared, not yet walkable: an AUTHORED proposal composed from this bundle's own assets; nothing here is built into the 3D world, no dimension is a measurement of a real venue, and no duration, price or accreditation is stated

`spaces/registry/spaces.json#honesty.composed_only_from`:

> - labels/registry/labels.json#kinds
> - props/registry/props.json#crib_furniture.pieces
> - props/registry/props.json#props
> - sims/registry/sims.json#sims
> - stations/registry/stations.json#stations
> - tools/registry/toolcribs.json#cribs
> - surfaces/registry/finishes.json#catalogue
> - surfaces/registry/finishes.json#wall_catalogue

`spaces/registry/spaces.json#honesty.cost`:

> a prediction summed from declared per-piece figures, not a measurement

`spaces/registry/spaces.json#honesty.envelope`:

> a space must fit the room of its strand in every serving hall, read from web/interiors.py plan_for exactly as web/build_3d.py lays it out (3 m per grid unit); the room is a functional programme, not a survey of a building

`spaces/registry/spaces.json#honesty.footprints`:

> an item with no size_m in its registry is recorded as footprint unknown and its overlap is not checked; nothing is assumed

`spaces/registry/spaces.json#honesty.not_stated`:

> no duration, price, accreditation or venue

`spaces/registry/spaces.json#honesty.ppe`:

> never typed: strand base PPE plus the PPE of every hazard the surfaces rules say the chosen floor and wall stand for in that strand's room; a hazard prop is admitted only when the finishes already derive a hazard it is keyed to
