# Skill: safety-and-limits

Safety and honesty limits for the SmartCiti.X learner platform. Apply these
before quoting any content to a learner, a bank, a lawyer, or an investor.

## Simulation and demo only

- Every scenario is **simulated training only — NOT certification,
  licensure, or clinical training**. Nothing here certifies anybody (the
  SmartCiti.X README says so itself).
- All progress data is **Demo mode sample data, not real learners**.
- VR practice is not clinical hours. Completed scenarios, XP, badges, and
  checklist ticks are never a credential or a completion record.

## Grounded sources (cite these, not the platform)

- OSHA Personal Protective Equipment topic page (osha.gov): PPE minimises
  exposure to hazards; workers must be trained on when it is necessary,
  what kind, how to put it on / adjust / wear / take it off, its
  limitations, and its care, maintenance, useful life, and disposal.
- EPA Section 608 (epa.gov/section608; 40 CFR Part 82, Subpart F, Clean Air
  Act): prohibits intentionally venting ozone-depleting refrigerants and
  their substitutes (e.g. HFCs) during maintenance, service, repair, or
  disposal. Technician certification programmes exist; this platform does
  not certify.
- CDC Guidelines for Infection Control in Dental Health-Care Settings, 2003
  (MMWR 52(RR17)): Standard Precautions, one-way instrument flow.
- SmartCiti.X wiki (org-wikis/SmartCiti.X/WIKI.md): 111-hall roster is an
  in-repo list, 0 of 111 signed off; 11 simulators; 10 authored campuses
  (not physical).

## Do not invent

- No partner, site, address, price, union, customer, or statistic beyond
  the cited sources. Louisiana + Indiana training sites are proposed/planned;
  no site, city, or address has been named.
- Never call a named hall a partner. Never quote "66-W3 series" (correct:
  33 named W3 sub-series, April 2026 internal list; SmartCiti.X is S10).
- Never sum suggested valuations, never present a suggested figure as real.
- SmartCiti.X is proposed, not incorporated / funded / offered.

## Device limits (do not claim otherwise)

- No real Quest / headset / phone run has been done in this environment.
  The Holodeck player link, joystick, and Enter XR flow are read, not
  proven on a device.
- The vr-entry page works from file:// and over HTTP; the scenario viewer
  works from file://. Neither has been rendered in a real browser here.
- If asked whether something works on a real device, answer: not verified
  here — needs Mike's device.
