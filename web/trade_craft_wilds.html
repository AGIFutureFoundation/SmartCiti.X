SmartCiti.X : Trade Craft Academy

powered by AGI Corp · the wilds: large exterior worlds with trade work sites, trails and hidden caches

Walk 4 authored worlds, 427.9 km² in all, with 23 trade work sites tied to 36 union halls and 51 lessons. Every landscape is generated from a seed: an authored landscape, not a survey.

W A S D or the arrow keys walk · drag to look · Q and E turn · Shift runs · O shows the overview · on a touch screen the stick walks and a drag looks · click the minimap to travel · walk onto a site to read about the work there.

Minimap: orange squares are work sites; a ringed square has simulated tasks.

Tasks are practice. A task list certifies nothing, qualifies nobody and enters no completion record; only the completion contract in completion/ decides what a record says, and it is unchanged by this pack.
