Robotics lab

Environment specs built from the simulators and worlds in this bundle, three authored robots you can drive, and a recorder that keeps a demonstration only when you ask. No model has been trained on any of it.

  • NOTHING HAS BEEN TRAINED. No model has been trained on these environments or on any episode they produce, and the repo records no training run. They are specs and a runnable schematic sandbox.
  • Arenas, embodiments and dynamics are AUTHORED / SCHEMATIC (unicycle kinematics, circle obstacles); not the dynamics of any real robot, vehicle or ROV.
  • Counts and limits named in `sources` are READ from sims/, ambient/, tasks/, restoration/, underwater/ and web/deepkit.py at build time.
  • Reference policies are SCRIPTED: hand-written, deterministic, seeded - no model, no network.
  • Shaped after gym-style environment specs. Not an RLDS, LeRobot or ML-Agents file: no round-trip test exists in the repo, so no compatibility is claimed.
  • Episodes (training/ kind world-teleop) are opt-in per episode, device-local, and carry no names, emails, free text, audio, camera, biometric, device id or real-world location. Classroom / K-12 mode offers neither recording nor sharing.

How a learner records a demonstration

  1. Pick an environment and a seed; the arena is laid out the same way for that seed every time.
  2. Press Record. Nothing is kept unless you do, and nothing is kept if the training recorder is switched off.
  3. Drive with W A S D or the arrow keys, or the on-screen pad on a phone. The pose, speed, nearby object ids and your inputs are sampled five times a second.
  4. When the task ends (or you press Stop) the episode is kept in this browser only, beside your other training episodes.
  5. Run the reference policy on the same seed to compare, export the file, and share it only if you choose, from the Contribute page.

What is and is not trained

  • Is: environment specs with units, ranges, reward terms and termination, read from this bundle’s registries.
  • Is: a scripted reference policy - hand-written, deterministic and seeded, with no model and no network behind it.
  • Is: demonstration episodes you choose to record, in this browser, in a documented shape.
  • Not: a trained model. No model has been trained on these environments or episodes, and none is claimed.
  • Not: a real robot. The robots and arenas are authored and the motion is schematic.
  • Not: an upload. Nothing leaves this browser unless you export a file; classroom mode offers no recording.

operator.boom-lift SCRIPTED

  • clip the harness to the basket anchor on the ground, before the first lift
