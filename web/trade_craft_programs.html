How this page is assembled

  • play_safe: a lesson is play-safe when its title, why and limits contain no word on the hive k12 hold list; a Cognition.X block when its theme, description and transfer check contain none; an agent line when its text contains none. Held items are counted, never reworded.
  • band_fold: a Cognition.X band joins the schools band whose grade range contains it (K = 0).
  • program_order: 1 the allow-listed Hive agents (advisors, then parish figures, hive order); 2 courses carrying play-safe lessons for the band (courses registry order, lessons in course order); 3 classroom modules whose lessons stand in the band (classroom order); 4 Cognition.X modules with play-safe blocks in the folded bands (cognitionx order).
  • cards: one card per band and classroom module of that band; a simulator seat is listed only when the band admits sims. What a class does = the band offer, then the schools unit home and class drill when the band admits stations, the floor sims when it admits sims, and the gate when the offer names the unaided verification gate.
  • greeter: the allow-listed k12-guide standing in the lowest-FIPS parish among the module places (lowest id); with none, the campus Orientation guide at the hall door. The line is the first play-safe greeting, say or knowledge line, in that order. In classroom mode the voice starts muted.
  • sectors: community health = the Smiles district; disaster response = respond/ frames, the halls they touch and the responder figures; humanitarian training = the parish UN training (PROPOSED) and Disaster relief paths and their trainer and relief roles; environmental restoration = restoration/ sites and scenarios, their trade needs and crews.
