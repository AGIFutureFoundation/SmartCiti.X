Classroom and scoreboard

Teachers build a class plan across the open worlds; learners meet lesson moments as they explore and keep their own scoreboard in their browser.

  • No server and no accounts: everything on this page runs in your browser.
  • A class plan is a file and a link. It is not access control: no server enforces it, and anyone can open any world.
  • Scores, XP, streaks and badges are play. They never enter a completion record and certify nothing.
  • No student data leaves the browser. A class scoreboard exists only when a teacher imports files that learners chose to export.
  • Lessons are unverified general practice, quoted from the lessons pack; K-12 districts are PROPOSED partners with no agreement.
