What these agents are

  • SCRIPTED: every line is quoted word for word from a registry, and its source is shown beside it.
  • No AI model: nothing is generated, and no network is reached.
  • Voice is your device’s own speech synthesis. It speaks only when you press Speak, never on its own.
  • Personalities are AUTHORED by hand. They change how a line is delivered, never what it says.
  • Every agent in the Omega Hive is SCRIPTED: each line it says is quoted verbatim from the registry path printed beside it. Nothing is generated, at build time or at view time.
  • No AI model runs behind any agent, and no network is reached. The council picks lines by counting shared words between a question and the quoted lines - a fixed rule that gives the same answer every time - and says so when nothing matches.
  • Voice is the device's own speech synthesis (the browser's speechSynthesis, on-device voices only). It speaks only when you press a speak button, never on its own, and a mute switch silences it. No audio is recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Personalities and voice settings are AUTHORED by hand for this roster; they change how a quoted line is delivered, never what it says.
  • No agent represents a real worker, teacher, ranger, pilot, union officer or public figure; names are the source registries’ own AUTHORED names.
  • Talking to an agent is play: it certifies nothing, unlocks nothing and enters no completion record. Lessons stay unverified general practice.

Classroom mode: only play-safe guides are shown, lines with held words are withheld, and the voice starts muted.

Voice

Speech uses an on-device English voice of this browser, because every line is quoted in English as its registry writes it. Nothing plays until you press a Speak button.

Council

Ask one question. Up to five agents answer in turn, each with the registry line that shares the most words with your question. The same question always gets the same council, and the hive says so when no line matches.
