The New Orleans parishes

A large walkable world of connected parishes: walk, drive or take a boat across shared borders.

W / S or the arrow keys move, A / D turn, Shift runs; drag to look. Walk or drive over a border and the next parish streams in. Boats stay on water.

What is real here

  • Flat AUTHORED ground at 0 m with water outside the land outline: no real elevation.
  • Buildings, trees and streets are AUTHORED procedural fabric, not the real street grid.
  • Satellite imagery is a view-time layer requested by your browser and never stored here.
  • Generic vehicle and watercraft types only: no makes, models, brands or logos. Every dimension, mass, speed, acceleration, turning and seat figure is AUTHORED - a plausible arcade figure typed for a generic type, not measured and not the specification of any real machine. Driving here is play, not training: only the entries linked to an existing training seat open that seat, and no drive in the fleet enters a completion record.
  • no real elevation: New Orleans elevation is not held by this pack; the ground is flat AUTHORED at 0 m
  • typed from general public record by the parishes pack author; no source was fetched; coordinates are approximate (about 100-500 m) and were never surveyed
  • RECORDED - U.S. Census Bureau 2017 cartographic boundary file via us-atlas 3.0.1 counties-10m (1:10,000,000, quantized; coarse: expect vertex error of hundreds of metres)
  • view-time only, in the learner's browser; never fetched, stored or baked by any build; the 4k maps contain no imagery
  • The Census cartographic boundary outline is clipped to the COASTLINE only: open water on the Gulf side (Lake Borgne, Breton and Chandeleur sounds, the Gulf of Mexico, open bays) lies OUTSIDE every land outline and is drawn as water. INLAND water is NOT cut out at 1:10m: Lake Pontchartrain, Lake Maurepas, the Mississippi River and marsh open water lie INSIDE the parish outlines (parish lines run through Lake Pontchartrain, so Orleans, Jefferson and St. Tammany share borders across open water). This pack holds no inland-water polygon: inside an outline is NOT proof of land. Water is stated, not surveyed; no shoreline here is navigational.
