Quests and arcade

Side quests, games, treasures and easter eggs across the whole academy, gated by the lessons they use.

This is play. Quests, games, treasures, easter eggs and badges are play: they are kept on this device only, they are never evidence of anything, and they never enter a completion record. Lessons stay unverified general practice, exactly as the lessons page says.

Start the main chain here.

Arcade

Each game is locked until the lessons it needs count as done on this device. A lesson counts when every step this device can record is done (walk and placard steps are recorded by nothing, so they are not waited on). The round clock and the pick sizes are AUTHORED game rules; the signals, keys, chart and rubric are the simulator seats' own.

For demos only. Off every time the page loads, kept nowhere, and a round won with it on is not recorded.

Signal caller

From the rigging-signals seat: You are the signalperson: the lift card calls the moves, and the crane follows YOUR hands. Give each called signal in order — a wrong signal counts against you and the crane holds — and finish the lift with the stop signal.

Locked. To unlock: walk "Call the first lift before anybody touches the hook", "Walk where the bundle will fly before it flies".

  • Call the first lift before anybody touches the hook
  • Walk where the bundle will fly before it flies

Chart or refuse

From the load-chart seat: Work the pick list against the chart on the board: at each radius the crane has one honest number. Hook the picks the chart allows and refuse the ones it does not — an overweight pick accepted is the failure that matters.

Locked. To unlock: walk "Refuse the pick the chart refuses".
