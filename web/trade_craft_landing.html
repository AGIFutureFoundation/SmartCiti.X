The mission

Skilled work is not a fallback. It is the foundation.

For two generations, trades training has been treated as the path for people who did not take the other one. That framing was always wrong, and it is about to become expensive. Data centres need electricians. Electrification needs line workers. Every climate commitment on earth is a promise about pipefitters, ironworkers and insulators who do not exist yet.

The AGI Future Foundation builds public-interest education infrastructure. Trade Craft Academy is its answer to a specific question: if intelligent systems can now teach, what should they teach first? Our answer is the work that cannot be automated away and cannot be learned from a video — skilled physical craft, taught to mastery, at a scale no apprenticeship system has ever reached.

What we hold ourselves to

  • Never boredom, never anxiety. Difficulty is throttled to the edge of current capability, continuously, per skill.
  • Every decision explains itself. A learner can ask why they were given any task and get the real reason, from the same record an auditor reads.
  • A ticket means something. Certification requires demonstrated, unaided work at gate difficulty. Help cannot buy it.
  • The system answers to people. Instructors override it, learners can ask for an easier day, and it stops adapting entirely rather than adapt badly.

How it works

An instrument, not a playlist.

Most “adaptive” learning is a fixed sequence with a difficulty knob bolted on. This is a control system. It estimates what you can do, serves work just inside that edge, and proves the estimate before it certifies anything.

The dial

A learner profile turns telemetry — accuracy, hesitation, help requests, control smoothness in the sim — into a proficiency estimate, and a closed-loop controller holds predicted success inside 0.70–0.85. Above the band is boredom; below it is anxiety. Both are failures, and both get corrected within a handful of tasks.

The graph

Skills are a graph, not a list. Evidence on one skill moves its prerequisites, confusable pairs are kept apart, and what you learned in March comes back for review before you lose it. When someone is stuck, the system diagnoses which prerequisite is missing rather than repeating the same task harder.

The gate

Practice runs at the middle of the band, where learning happens. Certification runs at the top of it, unaided, with hints closed — a deliberate verification run, because practice difficulty can never prove mastery. Certification is an awarded event on an append-only record.

The coaches

Every hall has a mentor agent that knows its trade. They coach, explain and escalate to a human instructor when something is wrong on site. What they cannot do is change your difficulty, your record or your certification — that is enforced in code, not asked for in a prompt.

For investors

Infrastructure, priced like infrastructure.

The workforce gap in skilled trades is not a marketing thesis; it is an arithmetic one. Retirements are outrunning apprenticeship intake in every industrialised economy, and the constraint on electrification and reindustrialisation is people, not capital.

What we have built is the part that is hard to build: a validated curriculum ledger, an adaptive control system that has been calibrated against simulation and hardened against long-run and adversarial failure, an agent layer whose safety properties are enforced rather than promised, and a security model designed for multi-tenant institutional customers.

What we have not built is a business with revenue. We are pre-launch, pre-pilot and pre-accreditation, and we would rather you learn that here than in diligence.

For educators & training directors

You keep the judgement. It keeps the bookkeeping.

An adaptive system should make an instructor more powerful, not redundant. Ours is built so that the decisions requiring judgement stay with you and the decisions requiring memory stay with it — who is stuck and on what, who is overdue for review, who is ready to be tested, and who has been quietly leaning on help for three weeks.

  • You can always override. Pin any learner to any level. The override is logged, and the system down-weights that evidence because it knows it is no longer a clean measurement.
  • You see the reasoning. Every task served carries the reason it was chosen. If you disagree with the system, you can see exactly what it believed and why.
  • Fairness is monitored, not asserted. Outcomes are compared across sites, languages, device types and age bands. A gap above 10% opens a ticket and blocks releases — and quietly tuning one cohort's settings to make the gap disappear is itself flagged.
  • It stops rather than guess. If calibration drifts or a cohort is struggling, adaptation halts and everyone gets fixed difficulty until a human has looked.

What a learner never loses

Data rights

Training records are held under a pseudonym; the link to a person lives in a separate service that no standing role can read. Erasure breaks that link, which is what makes it real rather than cosmetic.

Emotional-state signals — the flow and frustration estimates the dial uses — are the most protected data we hold, not the least. They are kept for 90 days, are never included in any export, and are never visible to an employer.

Education technology has a credibility problem because it routinely describes intentions in the present tense. Here is the actual state, and the same table goes to every investor, partner and institution that asks.

Seventeen protocol defects have been found and fixed by simulation, long-run soak and adversarial testing — including a certification path that could never actually certify anyone, a single malformed event that could permanently corrupt a learner's record, and a help-fading rule that was specified, believed to be in force, and had never once run.
