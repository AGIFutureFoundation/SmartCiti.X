The fleet showroom

Vehicles and watercraft: a showroom and test track

Every generic land vehicle and watercraft in the fleet registry, on one turntable. Pick one, read its specs, then take it to the test-drive pad or the test-float pond.

Driving here is play, not training: it never enters a completion record and certifies nothing.

Filter

    W/S or arrow keys: throttle and reverse · A/D: steer · Space: brake · Esc: back to the turntable

    Traffic follows AUTHORED lanes on the test pad and rings on the pond: invented lanes, not real streets.

    AUTHORED

    Generic types only: no makes, models, brands or logos. Every figure is AUTHORED for a generic type, not measured and not the specification of any real machine. Registry: fleet/registry/fleet.json (stamp fd6a0d9f7fa0c263): 50 land vehicles, 20 watercraft, 34 families, 6 linked to an existing training seat.

    Physics is an arcade approximation: gravity 9.81 m/s^2; friction, restitution, mass, buoyancy, drag and damage are AUTHORED. Crashes are play - not a crash test, not safety or engineering advice. Vehicles never strike people, NPCs, pets or animals. Registry: physics/registry/physics.json (stamp 9fb11b4e4bec8666).
