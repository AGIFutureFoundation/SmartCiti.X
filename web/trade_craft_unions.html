What this is, and what it is not

  • A union named here is a hall in the Academy's own taxonomy of trades. It is not a partner, sponsor or affiliate, it has not reviewed or endorsed this page, and no real local is named.
  • No apprenticeship hours, wages, credentials, membership or affiliation are stated for any hall. None is recorded, so none is shown.
  • Any tie to a real apprenticeship programme is PROPOSED: no agreement exists with any union, programme or employer.
  • Courses are AUTHORED by rule; lessons are unverified general practice, and finishing one certifies nobody.
  • Omega Hive advisors are SCRIPTED: no AI model. Your device's own speech synthesis speaks a quoted registry line, only when you click, with AUTHORED personalities.

The registries say, in their own words:

  • a taxonomy of skilled trades, not a roster of chartered locals; no union has reviewed or endorsed it unions/registry/unions.json#honesty.taxonomy
  • the Academy's own regional training structure across its planned campuses - not a claim about any real union's locals, chapters or jurisdictions; no local is named unions/registry/chapters.json#honesty.chapters
  • planned locations named for real cities; no site has been surveyed, no address is recorded, and no figure comes from any city's records unions/registry/campuses.json#honesty.siting
  • unverified general practice. The lessons a course lists have not been reviewed by a journey-level practitioner, and grouping them does not change that. courses/registry/courses.json#honesty.content
  • no course certifies, qualifies or permits anybody to do anything. Finishing every lesson in a course leaves a learner with exactly the standing they started with. courses/registry/courses.json#honesty.not_certification
  • DERIVED: each path is an order chosen by the rules printed under `rules`, over ids READ from the registries listed under `sources`. Nothing is fetched and no model runs.

How each path is derived

order
course, then its lessons, then its seats, then its tool crib, then its advisor, then its crews - one path per hall
course
courses/registry/courses.json#courses["trade.<hall slug>"]; the course must name the hall and agree with the union registry on name, focus and district
lessons
the trade course's outline, which the course rule orders by ladder depth, then the lessons registry order
seats
the trade course's seat_time.seats_bound, in registry order (every seat a lesson uses is among them)
crib
tools/registry/toolcribs.json#hall_bindings[<hall>].district names the crib; it must be the hall's own district
advisor
the first advisor named by the hall's lesson supplements (where.advisors), in ladder order; a hall whose lessons name none gets the Orientation guide, the advisor that stands at every hall's door
crews
every crew in agents/registry/crews.json whose halls list names the hall, in registry order, with its roles as hive/registry/hive.json ids; a crew's seat must be one of the hall's bound seats
district_rollup
unions/registry/districts.json order; halls in union registry order; seats, crews and advisors counted once each, in order of first appearance
cohort_rollup
unions.json#cohorts order; the hall count must equal the cohort's own count
